Why join

Before you ask.

Do I need a proper camera?

No. A phone is enough for most walks and critiques. We teach composition and light first, and gear later. Members often lend lenses and tripods to each other.

Is there a membership fee?

No! Membership is completely free. There are no membership fees, annual fees, or hidden charges. You can enjoy all our membership benefits without paying anything.

I am a complete beginner. Is that fine?

Yes. Most of us started that way. On every walk, newer members are paired with someone who has been shooting for a while.

How often do you meet?

We have a weekly Club Day where members come together to learn, practice, and share their passion for photography. During each session, we explore photography techniques, learn about composition and lighting, and take on exciting photography challenges to improve our skills together.

Who owns my photos?

You do. We ask for your permission before showing any frame on this site, in print or on social media.
